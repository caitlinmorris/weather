# Decision memo — hover attaches to transitions, not states

**Trigger:** During the N=2 pilot's first day, crystallized event blocks
(the field's only hover targets) went absent for hours: they formed only on
topic change or return-after-gap, and long continuous same-topic sessions
produce neither. A hover-anywhere fix was tried and rejected within hours —
making old weather interrogable read as surveillance ("what was dan doing
at 2:47pm" is a supervisor-shaped question).

**Decision:** Hover targets are crystallized events only, and the event
grammar widened to include **mode changes** (exploring→building→debugging→
polishing…) alongside topic changes and returns-after-gap. Blocks dissolve
into plain, uninspectable cloud after ~1h (EVENT_FRESH_H).

**The principle underneath** (rendering-layer sibling of audience-loud /
activity-quiet):

> **Changes are legible; ongoing states are just weather.**

"Dan shifted from building to debugging" is a tell-me-more-shaped moment —
the licensed-opener rung of the glance spectrum. A queryable log of past
states is monitoring. The field now structurally affords the former and
cannot answer the latter.

**Instrumentation note:** at the topic+ dial level, event hovers carry the
person's name — attribution remains a per-viewer setting, never a property
of the passive field.
